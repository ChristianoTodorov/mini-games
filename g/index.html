Feel free to choose any game to play.

Puzzle game 15

In this popular puzzle game the objective is to line up every number in the correct order from 1 to 15. To achieve even better time you have the option to play with your keyboard.

2048

The game 2048 is a simple game in which the player has to slide all the numbers in such directions, so he gets the number 2048 by combining two boxes with the same number.

Nonogram

In order to solve and win this game, your objective is to find every box. To do that, you have numbers in each row and column indicating the number of consecutive boxes occurring in the respective row or column.

Sudoku

Sudoku is an infamous game in which the ultimate objective is to fill all the numbers from 1 to 9 without any repetitions in a row, column or 3x3 grid. Alternatively you have the option to solve any sudoku with a click of a button.

Snake

The objective of this game is very simple. Eat as much apples as possible, but be careful not to bite yourself.

Minesweeper

Find every mine on the field. The numbers represent the number of mines adjacent to them.

Tic Tac Toe

In a game of Tic tac toe each player's objective is to place three of his symbols (X or O) in a line. Play against a friend or an AI player.
